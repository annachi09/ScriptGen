Bill Issuance Validator

Finds different patterns of accounts whose billing didn't complete cleanly, and generates the correction script for each.

Two patterns, one table: a Rate bill (ID_OFFERED_SERVICE 176) still being put to collection that's either blocking the account's next Electricity/Water bill, or belongs to a brand-new contract whose very first cycle bill is still pending.

ℹ️ Stuck Bill. Starts from every account with a pending notice (GCCOM_NOTICE_TMP, COD_STATUS = 5000NOTEMP, "For validation") on a bill still "En proceso de puesta al cobro" (ESTFAC0012), narrows to the ones where that billing period's only bill is a Rate (176) bill, then checks the SAME account's Electricity/Water (1 or 19) bill for any of the next up to 11 billing periods (RJ, 2026-09-14: confirmed live that a valid stuck bill can lag the Rate bill by more than one period — the closest matching period is shown when more than one qualifies). A row here means that next bill is sitting at ESTFAC0015 ("En espera de otros servicios") — blocked behind the Rate bill above it.
ℹ️ New Contract Match. A Rate (176) bill still invoicing (ESTFAC0012) with a pending notice, where that account's contract is still Active (ESTSC00002), its own start date (GCCOM_CONTRACTED_SERVICE.FROM_DATE) lands exactly on this bill's LAST_BILLING_DATE — a brand-new contract whose very first cycle bill is still pending — and it's the only bill in its own billing period and the only bill currently pending validation on the account.

Finds accounts whose services are all Terminated, checks each service's own final bill (the one dated its termination date), and flags accounts where those final bills don't all share the same billing period.

ℹ️ How this works. For each Terminated service (GCCOM_CONTRACTED_SERVICE.STATUS = ESTSC00004) whose account also has a pending notice (GCCOM_NOTICE_TMP, COD_STATUS = 5000NOTEMP, "For validation") — accounts with nothing pending review aren't shown — finds that service's own final bill: the GCCOM_BILL row dated exactly its termination date and either "En proceso de puesta al cobro" (invoicing, ESTFAC0012) or "Puesta al cobro" (invoiced, ESTFAC0005). Only accounts where every service is Terminated are shown. An account is Complete when every service's final bill already exists and already shares the account's target period (the latest billing period among its matched final bills); otherwise it Needs action — either a service's final bill period needs realigning, or a service has no matching final bill at all and needs investigating.
