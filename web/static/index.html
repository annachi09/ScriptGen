What it finds. Bills with ID_BILLING_PERIOD > 10000000228 that carry a SANITARY billing concept, are not Rebilled (ESTFAC0042) or Cancelled (ESTFAC0007), and whose TOTAL_AMOUNT is above 14,158. BASE = TOTAL_AMOUNT / 0.155. NISS comes from the account's Water contracted service.

Compared with the Water bill. A bill is only listed when its amount is greater than the Water bill of the same payment form (GCCOM_BILL.ID_PAYMENT_FORM) with the same billing period and billing date, on a Water (19) contracted service, not a credit note and not Cancelled / Rebilled. Several Water bills are summed. No Water bill = not listed.

Generate Fix Script

Only Water and Electricity duplicates are listed. For a Water duplicate, the Sanitary item to bill that shares its reading is cancelled too, and the rebilling activity gets 2 bills (Water + Sanitary - the Sanitary bill is the Billed Sanitary item with the same dates); Electricity has 1 bill. For every checked row: (1) the duplicate item to bill (the Anomalous one) is cancelled - STTOBILL00 → STTOBILL04; (2) its readings (GCCOM_READINGS_ITEMSTOBILL) go to Billed - READ_STATUS = 7000STSRED; (3) if its ready usage is > 0, a GCCOM_REBILLING_ACTIVITY + GCCOM_REB_ACTIVITY_INFORMATION is inserted In process (ESTREF0002), type Massive substitutive (TIPREF0005), for the bill of the Billed twin (customer = that bill's account), and that bill is set to Disputed (BILLING_STATUS = ESTFAC0010). With "Process by batch" the bill note goes in EREALB0005 (Recalculado Batch) instead of EREALB0001 (Reclamado). The state is re-read from the database right before generating.

Wrong Billed Consumption

Per bill of the billing period: calculation base vs READINGS_ITEMSTOBILL ready usage vs GCGT_RE_READING ready usage.

ℹ️ About this page

Starts from the bill. Every GCCOM_BILL of the chosen billing period (default: the current one) that is Invoiced/Generated and not a Credit Note.

Three sources of usage per bill: (1) Calculation base = SUM(GCCOM_BILLING_CONCEPT_DETAIL.CALCULATION_BASE) for concepts CONCSMO003 + CC210; (2) Ready usage (Reading ITB) = SUM(GCCOM_READINGS_ITEMSTOBILL.READY_USAGE) over the bill's items to bill; (3) Ready usage (RE_READING) = SUM(GCGT_RE_READING.READY_USAGE) of those readings. A bill is listed when any two differ.

Speed. A period has ~1.7M bills, so the scan runs in chunks in the background with a progress bar (a few minutes); you can cancel it.

The current billing period is selected - click Scan (runs in chunks, a few minutes).

Bill Issuance Validator

Finds different patterns of accounts whose billing didn't complete cleanly, and generates the correction script for each.

Two patterns, one table: a Rate bill (ID_OFFERED_SERVICE 176) still being put to collection that's either blocking the account's next Electricity/Water bill, or belongs to a brand-new contract whose very first cycle bill is still pending.

ℹ️ Stuck Bill. Starts from every account with a pending notice (GCCOM_NOTICE_TMP, COD_STATUS = 5000NOTEMP, "For validation") on a bill still "En proceso de puesta al cobro" (ESTFAC0012), narrows to the ones where that billing period's only bill is a Rate (176) bill, then checks the SAME account's Electricity/Water (1 or 19) bill for any of the next up to 11 billing periods (RJ, 2026-09-14: confirmed live that a valid stuck bill can lag the Rate bill by more than one period — the closest matching period is shown when more than one qualifies). A row here means that next bill is sitting at ESTFAC0015 ("En espera de otros servicios") — blocked behind the Rate bill above it.
ℹ️ New Contract Match. A Rate (176) bill still invoicing (ESTFAC0012) with a pending notice, where that account's contract is still Active (ESTSC00002), its own start date (GCCOM_CONTRACTED_SERVICE.FROM_DATE) lands exactly on this bill's LAST_BILLING_DATE — a brand-new contract whose very first cycle bill is still pending — and it's the only bill in its own billing period and the only bill currently pending validation on the account.

ℹ️ How this works. For each Terminated service (GCCOM_CONTRACTED_SERVICE.STATUS = ESTSC00004) whose account also has a pending notice (GCCOM_NOTICE_TMP, COD_STATUS = 5000NOTEMP, "For validation") — accounts with nothing pending review aren't shown — finds that service's own final bill: the GCCOM_BILL row dated exactly its termination date and either "En proceso de puesta al cobro" (invoicing, ESTFAC0012) or "Puesta al cobro" (invoiced, ESTFAC0005).
